# Product Overview

ShubhTalk is an astrology consultation platform that connects users with astrologers for personalized readings and services.

## Core Features

- **Astrologer Consultations**: Chat, voice, and video calls with astrologers
- **Kundli Services**: Birth chart generation, matching, and analysis
- **Horoscope**: Daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly predictions for all zodiac signs
- **Tarot Readings**: Multiple tarot reading types (daily, love, career, yes/no)
- **Panchang**: Hindu calendar and auspicious timing information
- **Calculators**: Numerology, compatibility, dasha, and various astrological calculators
- **Live Streaming**: Real-time astrology sessions with gift support
- **E-commerce**: Astrology products and services marketplace
- **Wallet System**: In-app payment and transaction management

## User Types

- **Customers**: Users seeking astrological guidance and services
- **Astrologers**: Service providers offering consultations (managed separately)

## Real-time Features

- Socket.io for chat and call management
- Agora SDK for voice and video calls
- Firebase for notifications and real-time updates
